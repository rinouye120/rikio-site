I am a postdoctoral fellow at [Stanford University's](https://www.stanford.edu/) [Center for International Security and Cooperation (CISAC)](https://cisac.fsi.stanford.edu/cisac-fellowships#fellowship-opportunities). Afterwards, in 2027, I will start as an Assistant Professor in the [Department of Political Science](https://liberalarts.temple.edu/academics/departments-and-programs/political-science) at [Temple University](https://www.temple.edu/).

My research examines how race, religion, and national identity shape public opinion about international conflict and foreign policy. I study how people evaluate countries and international actors, with particular attention to how racial and religious identities affect support for countries in conflict. I also examine how democracy, democratic backsliding, and alliance identities shape foreign policy preferences and public support for international cooperation.

My research has been published twice in *International Studies Quarterly* and accepted for publication at the *American Political Science Review* and *American Journal of Political Science*. Drafts and working papers are available on my [Research page](publication/).
